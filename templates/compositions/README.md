# Role-based composition seeds

See `reference/25-commercial-composition.md` for selection, limits and the native renderer contract. These are geometry budgets, not complete slides or the components.js catalog.

- `proof-boundary`: three inputs, an explicit verification stage and two deliverables
- `evidence-rubric`: six criterion/evidence pairs and five ordinal states, without invented service scores
- `offer-outcome`: one fee/duration/start/inclusions group paired with a purchase outcome

Bind only real source copy with `scripts/composition_layout.py`. Inspect `required_fields` in the chosen JSON. Color tokens and fonts come from the project. Register factual numbers with the author's format-specific fact registry. Render, inspect and compare before delivery. Other content counts or aspect ratios require a fresh composition; never fill absent data to satisfy a preset.

Matching the broad role is insufficient: every required field must exist in the brief. In particular, offer-outcome also requires scope/work notes and an unverified-scope condition. If they are absent, adapt the composition or choose another pattern; do not invent them.
